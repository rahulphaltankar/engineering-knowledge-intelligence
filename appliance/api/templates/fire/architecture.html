Governance controls

Evidence is data
Retrieved records are passed to any model as untrusted context, never as instructions.
Exact citations
Every cited record must exist; fabricated citations are detected and the claim downgraded or removed.
No invented criteria
An acceptance criterion not in the evidence is reported as not established; outputs that synthesise one are blocked.
Unknown is an answer
What the evidence does not establish is reported as UNKNOWN with no value assumed.
Lower trust for models
Model interpretation is bounded, classified INFERRED and below deterministic findings.
Human review
Finding-level decisions with a recorded reason ({{ review_label|lower }}).
